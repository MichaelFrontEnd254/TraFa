About TraFa

Curating contemporary design for mindful living

Our Story

Founded in 2018, TraFa emerged from a simple observation: contemporary living spaces deserved objects that were both beautiful and purposeful. We believe that every item in your home should earn its place through exceptional design and lasting quality.

Our curated collection represents the work of talented designers and artisans from around the world. Each piece is selected not just for its aesthetic appeal, but for its contribution to a more thoughtful way of living.

Our Philosophy

We champion architectural minimalism — the idea that form and function are inseparable. Every curve, angle, and material choice serves a purpose. Nothing is arbitrary. This philosophy extends beyond our products to every aspect of how we operate.

Sustainability isn't a marketing term for us; it's a fundamental principle. We partner with makers who share our commitment to responsible materials, ethical labor practices, and designs built to last generations rather than seasons.

Sustainability

Our environmental commitment includes sourcing from certified sustainable suppliers, using recyclable or biodegradable packaging, offering carbon-neutral shipping options, and partnering with artisans who employ traditional, low-impact production methods.

Shipping & Delivery

We offer free standard shipping on all orders over $500. Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days, while expedited shipping (2-3 days) is available for an additional fee. International shipping is available to select countries with customs fees calculated at checkout.

Returns Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery in their original condition. Custom or personalized items are final sale. Contact our customer service team to initiate a return.

Join Our Team

We're always looking for passionate individuals who share our vision for thoughtful design. From product curation to customer experience, every role at TraFa contributes to our mission of bringing exceptional objects into everyday life.

Current openings include positions in product curation, customer experience, warehouse operations, and digital marketing. Please send your resume and a brief introduction to careers@trafa-design.com
