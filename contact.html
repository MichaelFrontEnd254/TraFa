Frequently Asked Questions

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for items in their original condition with tags attached. Custom or personalized items are final sale. Return shipping is free for defective items; otherwise, customers are responsible for return shipping costs.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Expedited shipping (2-3 business days) is available for an additional fee. Free standard shipping is offered on orders over $500. International shipping times vary by destination.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to select countries worldwide. International shipping costs and customs fees are calculated at checkout. Please note that international orders may be subject to import duties and taxes, which are the responsibility of the customer.

Are your products sustainably sourced?

Sustainability is core to our mission. We partner with artisans and manufacturers who use responsible materials, ethical labor practices, and low-impact production methods. Many of our products are made from natural, renewable, or recycled materials.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes! We offer complimentary gift wrapping with a personalized message card. Simply select the gift wrap option at checkout and include your message in the notes section.

Can I cancel or modify my order?

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement. After this window, orders enter our fulfillment process and cannot be changed. Please contact us immediately if you need to make changes to your order.

Do you have a physical showroom?

Yes, our flagship showroom is located in Brooklyn, New York. Open Tuesday-Saturday, 11AM-7PM, and Sunday 12PM-6PM. Appointments are recommended for personalized consultations. Please contact us to schedule a visit.

Do you offer wholesale or trade pricing?

Yes, we work with interior designers, architects, and retailers. Please complete our trade application form or email trade@trafa-design.com with your business details and tax ID for more information about our trade program.
